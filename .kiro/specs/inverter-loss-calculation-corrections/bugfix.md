# Bugfix Requirements Document

## Introduction

This bugfix corrects the inverter-loss requirements and published conclusions without claiming stronger evidence than is available. It preserves the inverter-only boundary, prohibits unjustified hot-start assumptions, requires sensitivity tests that expose a real MOSFET change, and prevents loaded 36 W, 42.8 W, or similar constants from being described as unavoidable no-load loss. Every material result must distinguish engineering targets from direct manufacturer ratings, ideal commanded behavior from implementation residuals, firmware implementation from hardware measurement, and calculated estimates from validated facts. Firmware changes are outside this bugfix scope, and incomplete evidence must remain safely `PROVISIONAL` or `UNAVAILABLE`.

## Bug Analysis

### Current Behavior (Defect)

1.1 WHEN IRL40SC209 resistance is selected or reported THEN the system can present 0.59 mΩ or 0.72 mΩ IRL40T209 data as direct IRL40SC209 evidence, report 0.60 mΩ as a manufacturer rating, or accept or reject conflicting 1.3 mΩ and 1.7 mΩ fields without recording the exact part, document ID and revision, page or table, rating class, test conditions, and field-level adjudication.

1.2 WHEN 10%, 1%, or 0% phase commands are evaluated THEN the system can treat the percentage as self-defining, derive an acceptance oracle from the implementation under test, or omit the repository-grounded nominal 70 kHz label, actually compiled integer-derived tick/period/half-period semantics, comparator, edge-order, dead-time, synchronous-rectifier, load-state, stored-energy, or independently approved analog-reference requirements.

1.3 WHEN phase command reaches zero THEN the system can force actual differential effects and losses to zero, accept a caller-provided settled flag or locally chosen settling thresholds, omit or duplicate a physical residual source, fold measurement uncertainty into physical loss, or aggregate residuals without source-level provenance and availability.

1.4 WHEN preservation checking is run THEN the supported non-bug domain can be empty, contain only ineligible cases, omit a preserved or nested category, mark a category inapplicable ad hoc, fabricate a preservation observation where no legacy behavior exists, or use an unfrozen or non-reproducible legacy adapter.

1.5 WHEN end-to-end acceptance is run THEN a 48 V case or synthetic lower-voltage fixture can gate or support a real qualification or manufacturer claim without a named device and complete direct evidence for its voltage rating, calculated or measured stress, derating, operating conditions, and applicable qualification constraints.

1.6 WHEN inverter-only loss is solved THEN excluded battery, charger, C-rate, round-trip, or other upstream fields can enter through direct, transitive, or default dependencies, allowing a mutation to alter full-precision outputs, trace identity, or the dependency set even when the displayed total omits those fields.

1.7 WHEN a cold elapsed-time-zero snapshot, thermal derivative, elapsed-time transient, or steady-state solution is requested THEN the system can omit explicit physical initial temperatures, require thermal capacitance where it is irrelevant, omit positive evidenced thermal capacitance where dynamics require it, or substitute an undeclared hot condition.

1.8 WHEN materially different numerical seeds converge to agreeing thermal roots THEN the system can report uniqueness without a bounded root-isolation and root-count certificate covering the complete supported domain.

1.9 WHEN controlled exactly-threefold resistance, endpoint sensitivity, or realistic replacement-device losses are compared THEN the controlled checks can be skipped or run after the realistic comparison, the devices can use different fixed-temperature references, attribution buckets can overlap, or temperature-dependent electrical changes can be counted both in component deltas and in thermal feedback.

1.10 WHEN no-load control behavior is described THEN firmware implementation status and hardware measurement status can be collapsed into one status, allowing evidence in either dimension to imply evidence in the other.

1.11 WHEN the controlled exactly-threefold RDS(on) comparison is evaluated THEN the system can require a nonzero total-loss delta or a defined one-third ratio when Irms is zero or the conduction interval is empty, or apply the ratio without proving Irms greater than zero and nonempty conduction.

1.12 WHEN no-load, boundary, timing-reference, thermal, sensitivity, device-comparison, or preservation conclusions are published THEN the system can use locally chosen or code-selected policy values, multiple or dimensionally invalid tolerance sets, rounded acceptance values, non-exact boundary or digital-event comparisons, stale dependent values, loaded-state constants, unsupported precision, or calculated assumptions presented as unavoidable physical or measured facts.

### Expected Behavior (Correct)

2.1 WHEN IRL40SC209 is used without direct IRL40SC209 manufacturer RDS(on) evidence THEN the system SHALL store 0.60 mΩ with `EvidenceClass=USER_SPECIFIED_TARGET`, exact subject part `IRL40SC209`, and a user assertion artifact containing immutable artifact ID, version, and locator; the VGS, ID, Tj-or-Tc, and pulse-or-measurement-condition fields SHALL each contain exactly `VALUE(value, unit)` or `ABSENT`, and the value SHALL NOT be labeled typical, maximum, guaranteed, directly evidenced, or measured.

2.2 WHEN 0.59 mΩ or 0.72 mΩ from IRL40T209 is cited in an IRL40SC209 analysis THEN the evidence record SHALL contain exact subject part `IRL40T209`, manufacturer, manufacturer document ID, document version, immutable artifact hash, page and table-or-figure locator, rating class, and source-condition fields VGS, ID, Tj-or-Tc, and pulse-or-measurement condition, each encoded exactly as `STATED(value, unit)` or `NOT_STATED_BY_SOURCE`; it SHALL use `EvidenceClass=RELATED_PART` and SHALL NOT be promoted to direct IRL40SC209 evidence by a shared-die inference.

2.3 WHEN a source row associates 1.3 mΩ, 1.7 mΩ, or another conflicting resistance with the selected device THEN the system SHALL retain a separate raw field record containing every available subject, artifact ID, version, hash, locator, condition, value, and unit and explicit source-level absence markers. A raw unresolved row MAY temporarily omit `EvidenceClass` only while `ConflictStatus=PENDING`; such a row SHALL be ineligible for selection, acceptance, calculation consumption, qualification, or publication. Every non-pending row SHALL contain exactly one `EvidenceClass` from 2.4, and no row SHALL invent metadata.

2.4 WHEN any material device field other than the temporary raw unresolved row permitted by 2.3 is recorded or selected THEN `EvidenceClass` SHALL be exactly one of `USER_SPECIFIED_TARGET`, `DIRECT_MANUFACTURER`, `RELATED_PART`, `DERIVED_ESTIMATE`, or `MEASURED`, and `ConflictStatus` SHALL independently be exactly one of `NONE`, `PENDING`, `ACCEPTED_FOR_FIELD`, `REJECTED_FOR_FIELD`, or `SUPERSEDED`. The closed class-metadata matrix SHALL be: `USER_SPECIFIED_TARGET` requires subject, field, value/unit, assertion artifact ID/version/locator/hash, and user-condition fields each `VALUE` or `ABSENT`; `DIRECT_MANUFACTURER` requires exact subject, manufacturer, document ID/version/hash, locator, rating class, and source-condition fields each `STATED` or `NOT_STATED_BY_SOURCE`; `RELATED_PART` requires the same manufacturer metadata plus exact related subject and explicit relationship rationale; `DERIVED_ESTIMATE` requires derivation artifact ID/version/hash, formula or algorithm, all dependency record IDs, applicability, and uncertainty or bound; `MEASURED` requires test artifact ID/version/hash, instrument IDs and calibration versions, measurement boundary, conditions, sample coverage, method, and uncertainty. Fields required by the applicable class SHALL be present with the specified explicit absence encoding where permitted; metadata SHALL never be fabricated. A newly ingested record SHALL start `NONE` only when no conflict is known or `PENDING` when a conflict is known. The only permitted transitions are `NONE→PENDING`, `PENDING→ACCEPTED_FOR_FIELD`, `PENDING→REJECTED_FOR_FIELD`, and `ACCEPTED_FOR_FIELD→SUPERSEDED`; `REJECTED_FOR_FIELD` and `SUPERSEDED` are terminal, and later evidence SHALL create a new record. Each transition SHALL append adjudicator, timestamp, reason, and cited artifact IDs without mutating prior state; all other transitions SHALL fail validation. Exactly one record SHALL be selected for a subject-field-applicability tuple, its status SHALL be `NONE` when undisputed or `ACCEPTED_FOR_FIELD` after adjudication, and at most one non-superseded record MAY be `ACCEPTED_FOR_FIELD`; `PENDING`, `REJECTED_FOR_FIELD`, and `SUPERSEDED` records SHALL never be selected or consumed.

2.5 WHEN direct IRL40SC209 evidence is attached or a field is adjudicated THEN the system SHALL change only fields supported by cited exact-part evidence, apply only a transition permitted by 2.4, and preserve all prior records and provenance. Every `MaterialResult` that directly or transitively depends on the changed record SHALL be invalidated before the accepted selection changes; stale results SHALL be blocked from acceptance, qualification, headline use, dependent calculation, and publication until recomputed with a new trace identity. Required evidence or adjudication that remains unresolved SHALL propagate the global `PROVISIONAL` or `UNAVAILABLE` rules of 2.41 rather than a fabricated value.

2.6 WHEN phase magnitude p is used THEN the system SHALL define p as a real value from 0% through 100% of maximum commanded relative displacement between the two bridge legs; for a timing artifact with period count N and explicitly defined half count H, it SHALL define the ideal displacement in ticks as q = p × H / 100 and the ideal time displacement as φ = qΔt, where 0 ≤ q ≤ H. Output polarity SHALL be a separate value s ∈ {+1, −1}, where s = +1 makes leg A lead leg B by φ and s = −1 makes leg B lead leg A by φ. For the repository artifact, N MAY be odd and H SHALL equal floor(N/2); no exact T/2 or exact-50%-duty premise SHALL be inferred.

2.7 WHEN ideal and realized bridge timing is produced for this firmware THEN the system SHALL use the actually compiled integer semantics N = floor(INV_MCPWM_CLK_HZ / INV_CARRIER_HZ), H = floor(N/2), a leading raw high interval of H ticks, and its complementary interval of N − H ticks. It SHALL realize the commanded phase magnitude as k = clamp(floor(q + 0.5), 0, H), with q = p × H / 100 and exact half-tick ties rounded upward; any comparator guard, edge-specific quantization, dead-time rule, or implementation residual SHALL then be represented separately and exactly. A general timing artifact MAY be supported only when it explicitly defines H, the raw high and complementary interval lengths, phase quantization, and behavior for odd N; it SHALL NOT assume positive even N, an edge at exact T/2, or exact 50% duty unless those properties are explicitly true for that artifact.

2.8 WHEN a 10%, 1%, or 0% acceptance vector is defined THEN its digital portion SHALL reference the immutable `InverterOwner`-approved timing-oracle artifact from 2.36 by ID/version/hash and contain the nominal carrier label, exact clock, actual rational carrier, T, N, H, Δt, explicit odd-period behavior, p, s, ideal q and φ, realized k, raw high and complementary interval lengths, every comparator identity/value, every raw edge, every independently established dead-time-adjusted edge, canonical event identity/count/order for each available list, explicit `UNAVAILABLE(...)` records for any dead-time-adjusted edge behavior or complete list/count not independently established, dead-time ticks, minimum-window and enable rules, and source artifact locators. Any supported general timing artifact SHALL provide the same explicit H and odd-period semantics rather than inheriting an even-N or exact-50%-duty default. Its analog MODREF portion SHALL be a separate artifact approved by `IndependentModelReviewer` and identified by ID/version/hash, derivation independent of the implementation under test, primary/secondary polarity, DC-terminal voltage, load model/value, initial inductor/transformer current, closed initial stored-energy source set, carrier state, enabled power-stage states, and one 2.35 `EXPECTED(...)` or `UNAVAILABLE(...)` record for every intentional-transfer, differential-voltage, volt-second, RMS-current, circulating-current, flux, stored-energy, and loss field.

2.9 WHEN a 10%, 1%, or 0% vector is accepted THEN the system SHALL compare canonical expected digital ticks, all comparator identities/values, all raw edges and their event identities/count/order, and each independently established adjusted edge list and its event identities/count/order exactly against the fixed timing oracle. Any adjusted behavior or complete adjusted list/count marked `UNAVAILABLE` by the oracle SHALL remain safety-relevant but non-gating under 2.41 until the independent evidence required by 2.36 exists; it SHALL NOT be treated as an empty list, zero switching, zero gate transitions, or zero gate loss. The system SHALL compare canonical unrounded analog quantities only when their MODREF records are `EXPECTED(...)`, using only the approved tolerance in each field. The digital oracle SHALL NOT validate analog transfer, current, energy, or loss; any analog field with missing load, initial state, provenance, expected value, or tolerance SHALL be `UNAVAILABLE` and non-gating, and no available analog field SHALL imply availability of another.

2.10 WHEN p = 0 after settling THEN the system SHALL force only commanded ideal transfer and commanded ideal differential primary excitation to zero; it SHALL report actual differential pulses, residual RMS current, differential volt-seconds, flux, stored energy, and associated losses only from the typed residual-source records required by 2.37, or mark each missing applicable source and every dependent aggregate `PROVISIONAL` or `UNAVAILABLE`, and SHALL NOT substitute zero for an unknown residual.

2.11 WHEN a p = 0 case is classified `SETTLED` THEN the system SHALL evaluate a contiguous observation window W ≥ max(10T, 1 ms) using the complete product-threshold artifact required by 2.35. It SHALL evaluate Ires against the artifact's RMS method and sample coverage, absolute differential volt-seconds in every carrier period against VSperiod,max, absolute cumulative differential volt-seconds over W against VScum,max using the approved integration/alignment rules, and the end-of-W total over the artifact's closed stored-energy source set against Estored,max. Exact-value thresholds SHALL use direct comparison; interval thresholds SHALL use the artifact's approved conservative endpoint rule. Every comparison SHALL pass before `SETTLED` is available.

2.12 WHEN `ATS-v2`, a product-threshold artifact, any required observation, model, measurement, or its applicability is absent, invalid, non-finite, hash-mismatched, locally chosen, or out of scope THEN only the affected settled-state decision SHALL be `UNAVAILABLE` and non-gating under 2.41, and the p=0 case SHALL remain `TRANSITION`; when an available applicable limit is exceeded, the case SHALL be `TRANSITION` with a recorded failed comparison rather than `UNAVAILABLE`. In both cases the system SHALL retain actual or provisional residual and stored-energy losses, reject caller-provided settled flags, and SHALL NOT publish settled no-load behavior.

2.20 WHEN a cold snapshot at elapsed time t = 0 is requested THEN the input SHALL explicitly state finite physical initial temperatures for every modeled junction, case, heatsink, ambient, and coolant node as applicable; each temperature SHALL lie within the intersection of the declared component, material-model, and solver-supported domains. The case SHALL specify the Rth network, boundary temperatures, cooling mode, applied power versus time, initial physical temperature vector, elapsed interval, derivative/discretization and integration method, step-size or adaptive control, convergence/error controls, and numerical tolerances from an immutable artifact ID/version/hash approved by `InverterOwner`; absent or invalid thermal data, method, control, approval, or domain support SHALL make the derivative/transient `UNAVAILABLE`, not a cold snapshot or steady-state result.

For each Cth field unused by the steady equations, the invariance test SHALL independently replace that one field with at least two distinct positive finite in-domain values while holding all other inputs fixed, and every canonical steady-state output and dependency trace SHALL remain exactly invariant. 2.23 WHEN steady-state multi-seed checking is requested THEN the supported temperature domain SHALL provide finite lower-bound and upper-bound vectors, and the deterministic seed set SHALL contain exactly the lower-bound vector, componentwise midpoint vector, and upper-bound vector, deduplicated only when canonically identical. 2.24 WHEN a steady-state result is labeled `UNIQUE` THEN the system SHALL attach a bounded root-isolation and root-count certificate covering the complete declared parameter and temperature domain, proving the total admissible root count is exactly one and leaving no unresolved, unknown, or unclassified interval/box. 2.25 WHEN a realistic baseline-versus-replacement comparison is requested THEN comparison SHALL predeclare one finite `T_ref` with units, lying in the supported electrical-property and thermal domains of both exact devices, before either mandatory gate or the realistic comparison executes. The mandatory gate identities SHALL be exactly `CONTROLLED_THREEFOLD` and `ENDPOINT_SENSITIVITY`; each gate SHALL produce and freeze exactly one immutable pre-execution outcome artifact identified by artifact ID, version, and hash, no artifact or artifact identity SHALL be reused for both gates, and no additional gate-outcome artifact SHALL exist. Each artifact SHALL identify exactly one gate and contain exactly one outcome from that gate's closed union. The closed `CONTROLLED_THREEFOLD` outcome union SHALL be exactly one completed recorded result satisfying 2.29 or exactly one explicit ineligibility outcome permitted by 2.30 with code `INELIGIBLE_ZERO_IRMS`, `INELIGIBLE_EMPTY_CONDUCTION_INTERVAL`, or `INELIGIBLE_ZERO_IRMS_AND_EMPTY_CONDUCTION_INTERVAL`. The closed `ENDPOINT_SENSITIVITY` outcome union SHALL be exactly one completed recorded result satisfying the evaluable-case requirements of 2.31 or exactly one explicit ineligibility outcome permitted by 2.31 with code `INELIGIBLE_NO_POSITIVE_CURRENT_CASE`. Every ineligibility outcome SHALL contain exactly one code valid for its identified gate; state all and only the unsatisfied eligibility predicates corresponding to that code and a specific reason; record evaluated-case count exactly zero; and contain each field `completion_claim=false`, `pass_claim=false`, and `success_claim=false` exactly once. Wrong-gate, generic, unrecognized, or predicate-mismatched codes SHALL NOT satisfy either closed union and SHALL keep realistic comparison blocked. A valid ineligibility outcome SHALL permit only progression to realistic comparison; it SHALL never constitute or be represented, counted, summarized, traced, accepted, or published as gate completion, pass, or success. After both artifacts satisfy 2.38, comparison SHALL use complete corresponding baseline and replacement temperature vectors with identical node identities and units and the closed, mutually exclusive loss-category set `CHANNEL_CONDUCTION`, `GATE_DRIVE`, `EOSS_COSS`, `REVERSE_RECOVERY`, `SWITCHING_OVERLAP`, `INTERCONNECT`, `MAGNETICS`, `AUXILIARIES`, and `OTHER_DECLARED(subtype)`; every contribution SHALL map exactly once and every `OTHER_DECLARED` subtype SHALL be unique. Each fixed-temperature contribution SHALL be evaluated at T_ref, and the unrounded bucket sum SHALL reconcile to the fixed-temperature total delta within max(0.01 W, 1e-6 × relevant power), where relevant power is defined by 2.42.

2.26 WHEN thermal feedback is reported in that comparison THEN the system SHALL define it as [Lreplacement(Treplacement,converged) − Lreplacement(T_ref-vector)] − [Lbaseline(Tbaseline,converged) − Lbaseline(T_ref-vector)], using complete corresponding vectors and the same closed categories and operating boundary. The realistic total delta SHALL equal the fixed-T_ref total delta plus this thermal-feedback delta within max(0.01 W, 1e-6 × relevant power); no temperature-induced contribution included in thermal feedback SHALL occur in a fixed-temperature bucket.

2.27 WHEN no-load detection, automatic phase reduction, active standby, sleep entry, or another control behavior is reported THEN the system SHALL publish independent `firmware_implementation_status` and `hardware_measurement_status`, each exactly one of `VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `PROVISIONAL`, or `UNAVAILABLE`. `VERIFIED` SHALL imply 2.41 `AVAILABLE`; `UNVERIFIED` SHALL mean the applicable verification was executed on evaluable evidence but at least one named VERIFIED predicate failed, with every failure recorded; `PROVISIONAL` and `UNAVAILABLE` SHALL have exactly the global meanings in 2.41. `firmware_implementation_status=VERIFIED` SHALL require an immutable firmware source/config artifact ID/version/hash, named requirement-to-code trace, build/config hash, and passing deterministic test that exercises the complete requirement under the cited configuration. `hardware_measurement_status=VERIFIED` SHALL require an independent test artifact ID/version/hash, exact hardware identity, firmware/config hash, instruments/calibration, conditions, waveform/sample coverage, acceptance limits, and passing result for that same requirement. 2.28 WHEN firmware behavior is implemented but lacks qualifying hardware measurement, or hardware behavior is measured without matching qualifying firmware evidence THEN the system SHALL preserve the two differing statuses under 2.27 and SHALL NOT derive either from the other or publish a combined verified status; phase-command generation or PWM-stop capability alone SHALL NOT verify automatic no-load detection or control.

The sole `CONTROLLED_THREEFOLD` artifact SHALL retain its immutable artifact ID/version/hash, contain exactly one corresponding code, state all and only unsatisfied 2.29 eligibility predicates and a specific reason, record evaluated-case count exactly zero, and contain each field `completion_claim=false`, `pass_claim=false`, and `success_claim=false` exactly once. No other controlled-gate condition or code SHALL be an ineligibility outcome.

2.31 WHEN endpoint sensitivity is run for RDS(on) or another material device field THEN the sole `ENDPOINT_SENSITIVITY` artifact SHALL retain its immutable artifact ID/version/hash and use independently declared, evidenced, finite low and high endpoints whose canonical dimensions, units, and applicability match, with low < high; an available, valid, applicable policy; a predeclared closed candidate-case set; and every other canonical full-precision input fixed exactly across candidates and endpoints. 2.32 WHEN no-load loss is reported THEN each closed category `INTENTIONAL_IDEAL_DIFFERENTIAL_TRANSFER`, `UNINTENDED_DIFFERENTIAL_RESIDUAL`, `COMMON_MODE_SWITCHING`, `GATE_DRIVE`, `CONTROL_SENSING`, `FAN_POLICY`, `AUXILIARIES`, and `SLEEP_ENABLED_LOADS` SHALL have three separate fields: `applicability` exactly `APPLICABLE` or `NOT_APPLICABLE(reason, topology_control_matrix_cell)`; `result_basis` exactly `CALCULATED_ESTIMATE`, `MEASURED`, or `NONE`; and `availability` under 2.41.
